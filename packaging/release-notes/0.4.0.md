## 0.4.0

**手机 / Phone**
- 新增「设置 → 手机」：配合手机端 AwyRead，手机和电脑连同一个 Wi-Fi 时，把应用使用时长、步数、心率、睡眠加密传过来，和电脑活动一起整理成记忆。默认关着，打开后用 12 位配对码配对。
  New "Settings → Phone": together with the AwyRead phone app, app usage time, steps, heart rate and sleep are sent here encrypted whenever the phone and this computer share a Wi-Fi network, and organized into memories with your computer activity. Off by default; pair with a 12-character code.
- 手机回家才上传的数据也不会漏：整理时按「新收到的」写一份「手机活动摘要」。
  Data that arrives late (when the phone gets home) is not missed: each organizing run writes a "phone activity summary" of what was newly received.

**界面 / Interface**
- 新图标：程序、安装包和托盘都换成了新的角色图。
  New icon for the app, the installer and the tray.
