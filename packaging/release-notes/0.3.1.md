## 0.3.1

**界面 / Interface**
- 首页「时间线」可以切换从早到晚 / 从晚到早，会记住你的选择。
  The Today timeline can be shown oldest-first or newest-first, and remembers your choice.
- 聊天输入框贴到窗口底部，聊天区域更大。
  The chat input now sits at the bottom of the window, leaving more room for messages.
- 设置页「位置」「浏览器扩展」「当前版本」几项的按钮不再挤成一条窄竖条。
  Buttons under Location, Browser extension and Current version in Settings no longer get squeezed into a narrow column.

**记忆 / Memory**
- 聊天时附上的记忆，一天只算一次「被想起」，「越常被想起越牢」重新有了区分度。
  Memories attached to chats now count as "recalled" at most once a day, so frequently recalled memories stand out again.
- 用 DeepSeek 聊天时提示词更稳定，更容易命中缓存，更快也更省钱。
  DeepSeek chat prompts are more stable, so they hit the cache more often — faster and cheaper.
- 「一起聊过」不会再把你亲口说的事改标成「一起聊过」。
  "Talked about together" no longer relabels things you said yourself.

**更稳、更注意隐私 / Safer and more private**
- 启用记忆存档时如果中途断电，下次启动会自动换回原来的记忆；换回失败后又用了一阵的话，再换回前会先把这段时间的内容另存一份。
  If the power goes out while switching memory archives, the original memories are restored on the next start; anything recorded in between is saved separately first.
- 关掉「聊天内容参与整理」后，还没整理的聊天和 AI 自己留的便条都不会再交给整理 AI；再打开时不会补读关掉期间的聊天。
  With "Use chats in organizing" off, pending chats and the AI's own notes are no longer sent to the organizing AI, and turning it back on doesn't backfill the gap.
- 导出时人物、地点的名字和别名也会打码。
  Exports now also mask names and aliases of people and places.
- 聊天记录里有特殊换行字符、或者存档里有坏掉的聊天文件时，不会再让聊天页或整理出错。
  Unusual line-break characters or damaged chat files no longer break the chat page or organizing.
- 「原始记录保留天数」只能填 1 到 3650，填错不会再删掉当天的记录或让定时整理停掉。
  Raw-record retention is limited to 1–3650 days; bad values can no longer delete today's records or stop scheduled organizing.
